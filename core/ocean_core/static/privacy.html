Ocean

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Ocean is a personal AI assistant built and used by Gudani Mashanyu in Johannesburg, South Africa. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what Ocean does with data, including data from Google accounts.

What Ocean stores

  • Chats, uploaded files, pictures and memory notes, in Ocean's own database.
  • For a connected Google account: the account's email address and an encrypted sign-in token. Ocean does not copy or keep emails or calendar events; it reads them only when asked in a chat.

How Ocean uses Google data

  • Gmail: to search and read email when asked, and to save drafts in the Drafts folder. Ocean never sends email.
  • Google Calendar: to list events when asked, and to add an event only after the account owner taps Add.
  • Email and calendar text is passed to the AI service answering that chat, only in private chats, and only with providers whose terms allow confidential data.
  • Google data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models by Ocean.

Ocean's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnect Google in Ocean's Settings, or remove Ocean at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.
