Folders Ocean may use

Ocean can read files in these folders, and save files there after you approve each one. It cannot see anything else on your laptop, and it can never delete, move or rename files.

    Laptop brain (Ollama)

    Checking...

    Your GTX 1650 has 4 GB, so small models work best: llama3.2:3b, qwen2.5:3b or phi3:mini.

    To set it up:

    1. Install Ollama from ollama.com/download and open it.
    2. Open Terminal and run: ollama pull llama3.2:3b
    3. In Ocean, choose My laptop in the brain picker at the top.

    Updates

    Ocean checks for updates by itself.

    Start with Windows

    Shortcut

    Press Ctrl + Shift + O anywhere to show or hide Ocean. Closing the window keeps Ocean in the tray; right-click the tray icon to quit.
